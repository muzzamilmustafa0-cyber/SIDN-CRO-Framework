\subsection{Deployment in Production and Logistics Planning}
\label{ssec:deploy}

A weekly deployment of \SIDNCRO needs three data streams that planning
organizations already maintain: sales by product and customer segment with the
prices charged, cooperative-advertising spend by echelon from marketing
records, and recycled-input shares from procurement records. The structural
parameters are estimated from the variation in these historical decisions, so
the elasticity estimates are only as good as that variation; planned price
tests or rotating promotions are the practical way to supply it
(Proposition~\ref{prop:elasticity}). The workflow has four steps: retrain the
network on the latest history (8--17~seconds on one processor thread for the panels
of the benchmark); recompute the conformal quantile on the most recent weeks,
including the errors observed at last week's implemented plan (under
0.1~seconds); solve the robust plan (1--35~seconds per week); and set the
conservatism level $\rho$ according to the cost of violating capacity or
recycled-input commitments. Because the band and the
structural parameters are explicit, planners can audit the elasticities and
the width of the band before committing a plan.
